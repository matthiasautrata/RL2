# RL2 Project Context

## Domain

RL2 (Rights Language 2) — a policy language for digital rights and data governance. Semantic superset of ODRL 2.2 with operational semantics.

**Standards foundation:** RDF, OWL, SHACL, ODRL, PROV, related W3C specs.

**Experience and personas assumed:** Ontology design, policy design, data governance, regulated environments, legal/normative concepts, operational semantics.

**Formal methods used:** Semantics, logics, proofs, verification, type theory. Tools: WhyML, Lean, and similar.

## Working Style

**Thinking:** Skeptical and questioning. Never accept anything at face value. Always ask: Does this make sense? Is it logical? Is it defensible? Probe boundaries and edge cases.

**Design:** Minimal. Prefer generics and parameters over verbosity. Strong distaste for redundancy. If something can be derived, don't declare it. If two things are the same, unify them.

**Writing:** Concise and precise. No filler. Every word earns its place.

**Mindset:** Think like a mathematician — precise and valid. Think like an engineer — make it work, make it last, make it good.
